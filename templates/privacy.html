Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 Jan 2026

Overview

Jippy Store (“we”, “our”, “us”) provides a premium grocery browsing and ordering experience. Your privacy is our highest priority. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the robust choices you have regarding your personal information.

Data we collect

Device info

Model, OS version, and technical logs for smooth app performance.

Precise Location

Enabled only with your permission to show nearby availability.

We do not collect sensitive personal data unless explicitly provided by you. Payment details are handled by PCI-compliant trusted providers; we never store your card details.

How we use data

  • Service delivery

    Processing orders and showing relevant stock in your area.

  • Performance & security

    Preventing abuse, detecting errors, and enhancing app stability.

Sharing

We never sell your data. Sharing is limited to essential partners like hosting and payment gateways.

Your Choices

Toggle location settings or request a full copy/deletion of your data at any time.
